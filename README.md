# 📖 Project Overview

The **India Crime Intelligence Dashboard** is an end-to-end Business Intelligence project that analyzes Indian crime data from **2001 to 2013** using **Microsoft Excel** and **Power BI**.

The project demonstrates the complete analytics workflow, including **data cleaning, transformation, data modeling, DAX calculations, KPI development, and interactive dashboard design**. It enables users to explore crime trends across **States, Districts, Years, and Crime Categories** while providing valuable insights into crimes against women, regional crime distribution, and year-over-year growth.

The solution combines the analytical capabilities of **Excel** with the visualization power of **Power BI**, transforming raw crime records into meaningful business intelligence for better decision-making.

# 🧹 Data Cleaning

Before building the dashboards, the dataset was cleaned and standardized to improve accuracy and consistency.

### Data Cleaning Steps

- Removed duplicate records.
- Standardized inconsistent State and District names.
- Corrected naming inconsistencies such as A&N Islands and D&N Islands.
- Removed unnecessary aggregate rows (ZZ Total, Delhi UT Total).
- Corrected formatting inconsistencies.
- Validated numerical values.
- Verified missing values.
- Standardized the overall dataset structure.
- Prepared the dataset for Power BI data modeling.

# 📈 Key Insights

- Maharashtra records the highest overall crime volume across the study period.
- Uttar Pradesh consistently reports one of the highest murder case counts.
- Theft is the most frequently reported IPC crime category.
- Crimes against women show a continuous increasing trend over the years.
- Women kidnapping cases have increased significantly during the study period.
- Year **2013** recorded the highest overall crime volume.
- Dacoity cases have gradually declined, while Theft and Robbery continue to rise.
- Highly populated states contribute a larger share of national crime.
- District-level analysis highlights concentrated crime hotspots requiring targeted interventions.
- Interactive dashboards enable dynamic analysis by State, District, Year, and Crime Category.
